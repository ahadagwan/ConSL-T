See it work

A few facts in, the expert work out

Tell ConSL-T what the plant looks like. It works out the targets, finds what falls short, and proposes what to do about it, with the reason written next to every answer.

1You describe the plant

A small water works

  • A vendor reaches the site through a remote-access gateway.
  • The historian and patch server sit in a DMZ behind it.
  • Plant control links to the DMZ, with no firewall recorded on that link.
  • A compromise of plant control could stop treatment.
2ConSL-T works it out

Targets, with their reasons

Remote accessSL-T 2
Connected straight to the internet.
Plant DMZSL-T 2
Right behind remote access, no firewall on the link.
Plant controlSL-T 3
Reachable from the internet without crossing a firewall, so its separation target goes up a level.
Network segmentation for plant control: not met. Nothing recorded separates it from the DMZ.
