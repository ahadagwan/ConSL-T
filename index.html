An ISA/IEC 62443 implementation workbench for asset owners

ConSL-T takes any industrial or OT site from its first asset inventory to audit-ready deliverables. It works out the answers instead of handing you forms to fill in: security targets, applicable requirements, gaps, risks and a plan to close them.

Any OT siteWorked examples included: water, power, oil & gas, pharma, manufacturing
The workflow

From inventory to audit, in one place

Each step feeds the next, so a change in the plant shows up everywhere it matters.

Model the plant

Assets, zones and conduits

Capture the inventory on site or import it from a spreadsheet, with fields that fit each kind of equipment. ConSL-T proposes a zone and conduit design you refine.

Set targets

Security levels, derived

Each zone's target security level is worked out from consequence and threat, using a transparent, versioned method. Zones exposed to outside networks, or reachable through unprotected links, are raised automatically.

Assess

Requirements and gaps

See which system requirements apply to each zone, with many judged automatically from the controls you have recorded, and the gap between what you have and what you need.

Analyse risk

A guided risk workshop

Threat scenarios seeded from MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, rated against your organization's own risk criteria, with credit for countermeasures and a risk register that checks every decision against tolerance.

Plan

Mitigation and roadmap

Gaps are ranked by severity and turned into a costed plan and a dated roadmap, with a projection of how much each action improves your security posture.

Run the programme

The organizational side

Policies, training, change records, reviews and suppliers are read to judge the security programme and its maturity, and the regulations that apply to your sector are identified.

Automation

The automation does the heavy lifting

Most 62443 work is reading the plant and applying the standard's rules to it, again and again. ConSL-T does that reading for you, writes its answer, marks it as automatic, and leaves the final say with you.

Automatic

Targets worked out for every zone

Security-level targets are derived from what a compromise could do to people, the environment, production and the business, and from the threat the zone faces. Zones exposed to the internet, a vendor's remote access, or sitting behind such a zone without a firewall between them are raised on their own.

Automatic

Requirements judged from your controls

Record the firewall, the backup, the monitoring or the login policy once, and every requirement it answers is judged across every zone it covers, with the reason written out. Where a record shows only part of the picture, it says what is missing.

Automatic

A plan proposed for you

ConSL-T works backwards from your gaps to propose the actions that close them, names the zones and machines each one touches, separates real changes to the plant from simply writing down what is already true, and shows how much each action improves your posture.

Automatic

The programme read from your records

Policies, training records, change records, review plans and supplier assessments are read to judge the organizational requirements and how mature each one is: written down, being done, or done and repeated.

Automatic

Threats and regulations identified

Each zone gets the ICS attack techniques that apply to what it holds and how it is reached, and each site gets the regulations its sector and location bring with them, from NIS2 to OSHA process safety.

Automatic

What to record next

Where the engine cannot decide, it says exactly which fact on which machine would let it, grouped so one visit to site answers many questions at once.
